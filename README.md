> **Fast, private, offline-first Point of Sale and Inventory Management software engineered specifically for retail and clinical pharmacies.**


## 📋 Overview

**PharmaCare** is an offline-first desktop application designed to streamline daily pharmacy operations. It eliminates checkout friction at busy retail counters with rapid keyboard-driven billing, provides expiry warnings to prevent stock write-offs, and delivers actionable sales analytics—all powered by a local, secure SQLite database.


## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### ⚡ 1. Speed POS & Counter Billing
- **Instant Search**: Find medicines in milliseconds by brand name
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Complete end-to-end checkout workflows entirely from the keyboard during rush hours.
- **Invoice Printing**: Native support for full-page standard PDF invoices.
- **Tax & Discount Handling**: Built-in GST/tax calculation, line-item discounts, and flat bill markdowns.

### 🎯 2. 3-Tier Stock & Expiry Radar
- **Expiring Soon Radar**: Automated early warnings for batches approaching expiration (configurable 30/60/90-day threshold).
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Identifies medicines falling below minimum thresholds.
- **Out of Stock Prevention**: Prevents counter stockouts with clear status flags and reorder suggestions.


### 📦 3. Inventory & Bulk Operations
- **CSV Bulk Import**: Import thousands of medicine records in seconds.
- **Data Export**: Export complete stock levels, sales history, and audit reports to CSV for spreadsheet analysis.
- **Stock Adjustments**: Log damaged goods, supplier returns, or physical inventory audit discrepancies.

### 📊 4. Sales Analytics & Financial Insights
- **Daily Sales Charts**: Visual graphs showcasing daily revenue trajectories and peak counter hours.
- **Gross Margin & Profit Tracker**: Automatically compute margins per sale, category, and time period.
- **Top Performer Insights**: Identify fast-moving pharmaceuticals and slow-moving capital.

### 🔒 5. 100% Offline SQLite Data Architecture
- **Zero Latency**: All operations execute locally against a SQLite database with instant response times.
- **No Internet Dependency**: Full counter functionality without requiring an active internet connection.
- **Optional Cloud Sync**: Periodic & One-click cloud backup and sync integration (e.g., Dropbox) for secure offsite data redundancy.

### 🎨 6. Store Customization & Receipts
- **Custom Receipt Header**: Add pharmacy name, contact details, drug license number, and GSTIN to receipts.
- **Visual Themes**: Built-in dark and light themes designed for eye comfort during long working shifts.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Data Security

- **Local Storage**: All patient records, medicine pricing, sales invoices, and customer phone numbers remain strictly stored on your local machine.
- **No Telemetry**: No third-party data tracking or external data harvesting.
- **Backup Redundancy**: Database backups can be saved locally or synced to encrypted personal cloud storage.
